Warm Home Discount 2026 to 2027

The Warm Home Discount is a one-off £150 off your electricity bill (or a top-up voucher if you prepay). The scheme reopened in October 2026 for winter 2026 to 2027. In England and Wales, eligible households on Pension Credit or qualifying means-tested benefits usually get it automatically, after a data match between the government and suppliers. In Scotland, you may need to apply to your supplier. Read our news guide: who gets the Warm Home Discount this winter.

Sources: GOV.UK: Warm Home Discount.

Winter Fuel Payment and Cold Weather Payment

The Winter Fuel Payment is £100 to £300 for people born on or before 27 June 1960, paid automatically to most people in November or December 2026. If your taxable income is over £35,000, HMRC takes it back. In Scotland, the Pension Age Winter Heating Payment replaces it.

A Cold Weather Payment is made when the temperature in your area is recorded or forecast to be 0°C or below for seven days in a row, if you get certain benefits.

Sources: GOV.UK: Winter Fuel Payment · GOV.UK: Cold Weather Payment.

Grants for heat pumps, insulation and solar

  • Boiler Upgrade Scheme: £7,500 towards an air or ground source heat pump in England and Wales, with a temporary £9,000 rate for eligible homes on oil or LPG until 31 March 2027. No income test. See our heat pump guide.
  • Warm Homes Plan: the government’s programme for home upgrades, including grants delivered through councils for lower-income households and planned low-interest loans for solar panels, batteries and heat pumps.
  • ECO: the Energy Company Obligation, funded through bills, is ending as its last projects finish; support is moving to direct government grants.

Sources: GOV.UK: Boiler Upgrade Scheme · GOV.UK: Warm Homes Plan.

If you’re struggling to pay

  • Talk to your supplier early. Suppliers must offer affordable repayment plans and consider your ability to pay.
  • Ask about hardship funds. Many suppliers run grant schemes to clear energy debt.
  • Energy debt relief: Ofgem has set up a debt relief scheme to write off some historic energy debt built up during the energy crisis for eligible low-income households. Suppliers identify and contact eligible customers.
  • Prepay protections: you can ask for emergency and friendly-hours credit, and suppliers must not force-fit prepay meters in vulnerable households.
  • Free advice: Citizens Advice and National Debtline can help.

Sources: GOV.UK: help with energy bills · Citizens Advice: energy supply.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the Warm Home Discount?
In England and Wales most eligible households get it automatically and receive a letter. In Scotland you may need to apply to your supplier. It’s £150 off your electricity bill.
Can I get a free boiler?
Free boiler schemes through ECO are winding down. The Boiler Upgrade Scheme gives £7,500 towards a heat pump, not a gas boiler. Lower-income households should check Warm Homes Plan grants through their council.
Who gets the Winter Fuel Payment in 2026?
People born on or before 27 June 1960, living in England or Wales. If your income is over £35,000, HMRC takes the payment back. Scotland has a separate payment.
